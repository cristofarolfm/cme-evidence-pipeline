Background and burdenStandard first-line chemotherapy for advanced or recurrent endometrial cancer has been paclitaxel plus carboplatin .PMID:36972022OK
Background and burdenThe Cancer Genome Atlas described four molecular subtypes of endometrial cancer with different prognoses, and a simplified classifier groups tumors as POLE, mismatch repair deficient, p53 abnormal and no specific molecular profile .PMID:36399812OK
Background and burdenIn a Canadian cohort of 1357 evaluable patients, 380 tumors (28.0%) were mismatch repair deficient .PMID:36241860OK
Current evidenceIn the RUBY trial, 494 patients were randomized to dostarlimab or placebo plus carboplatin-paclitaxel, and 118 (23.9%) had dMMR/MSI-H tumors .PMID:36972026OK
Current evidenceIn the dMMR/MSI-H population, progression-free survival at 24 months was 61.4% with dostarlimab versus 15.7% with placebo (hazard ratio 0.28) .PMID:36972026OK
Current evidenceAt the second interim analysis, RUBY met its overall survival endpoint in the overall population (HR 0.69), with a larger reduction in the risk of death in the dMMR/MSI-H population (HR 0.32) .PMID:38866180OK
