Background and burdenGlioma is the most common primary brain tumor and is often associated with treatment resistance and poor prognosis .PMID:37458855OK
Background and burdenStandard treatment typically involves radiotherapy and temozolomide-based chemotherapy, both of which induce cellular senescence .PMID:37458855OK
Background and burdenSenescent cells remain viable and secrete a large number of factors, known as the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP), that paradoxically have pro-tumorigenic effects .PMID:37458855OK
Background and burdenGlioblastoma due to recurrence is clinically challenging, with 10-15 months overall survival .PMID:39021199OK
Background and burdenRadiation also harms the normal brain: whole brain irradiation causes progressive cognitive dysfunction in ~50% of surviving patients .PMID:31960269OK
Current evidence**Senescent tumor cells.** Irradiated GBM cell lines and patient-derived xenograft cultures senesce rapidly in a p21-dependent manner and secrete SASP factors, prominently IL-6, that activate JAK-STAT3 and NF-κB signaling in non-senescent GBM cells .PMID:39972068OK
Current evidenceSMAC mimetics targeting cIAP2 act as senolytics in senescent GBM cells, and the SMAC mimetic birinapant given as an adjuvant after radiotherapy prevented the emergence of recurrent tumors in PDX and immunocompetent mouse models .PMID:39972068OK
Current evidenceTemozolomide-induced therapy-induced senescence in glioma cells is determined by the p21CIP1-CDK1/2 axis and does not require activation of the DREAM complex .PMID:40319068OK
Current evidenceResidual senescent GBM cells activate a PERK-mediated unfolded protein response, and PERK inhibition acted as a senolytic and prevented recurrence in vitro and in vivo .PMID:39021199OK
Current evidenceChemotherapy-induced senescent glioblastoma cells had a tumor-promoting function and reduced survival in male mice through DDX58-mediated STAT1 signaling .PMID:40259469OK
Current evidence**Senescent cells in the irradiated brain.** After cranial irradiation, widespread senescence occurred in the mouse brain, with astrocytes being particularly susceptible, and senescent astrocytes secreted HGF to activate Met in glioma cells .PMID:34580063OK
Current evidenceThe senolytic drug ABT-263 (navitoclax) selectively killed senescent astrocytes in vivo and significantly attenuated growth of glioma cells implanted in preirradiated brains .PMID:34580063OK
Current evidenceSenescent astrocytes also promoted an immunosuppressive microenvironment by enhancing recruitment of myeloid inflammatory cells, and senolytic agents given after radiotherapy substantially prolonged survival in preclinical models .PMID:38342629OK
Current evidenceIL6 was the predominant SASP factor elevated in irradiated astrocytes, and ABT-263 or tocilizumab abrogated JAK2/STAT3 activation in GBM cells .PMID:41385178OK
Current evidenceAll-trans retinoic acid eliminated senescent astrocytes, improved survival in GBM-bearing mice and alleviated radiation-induced cognitive impairment .PMID:39513361OK
Current evidence**Protecting normal brain function.** In mice given fractionated whole brain irradiation (5 Gy twice weekly for 4 weeks), depleting senescent cells with ABT263/navitoclax at 3 months rescued neurovascular coupling and improved cognitive performance .PMID:31960269OK
Current evidenceIn the same irradiation model, navitoclax eliminated senescent endothelial cells and decreased blood-brain barrier permeability .PMID:37642933OK
Current evidenceRadiation-induced pericyte senescence contributed to blood-brain barrier dysfunction and glioma growth, and senolytic drugs significantly mitigated radiation-induced cognitive dysfunction .PMID:39110121OK
Current evidence**Other tumor types and agents.** In H3K27M-altered diffuse midline glioma models, irradiation induced senescence and navitoclax acted as a senolytic primarily via Bcl-xL inhibition .PMID:41430412OK
Current evidenceSenescent pilocytic astrocytoma cells upregulate BCL-XL and depend on it for survival .PMID:35977048OK
Current evidenceTemozolomide-induced senescent U87MG cells became sensitive to DR5-B-mediated apoptosis, but p53-mutated, MGMT-proficient T98G cells did not .PMID:41249587OK
Current evidence**Clinical landscape.** The only registered study identified is an early phase 1 trial combining dasatinib, quercetin and fisetin with temozolomide, LMP744 and an autologous vaccine in previously treated glioma with residual disease, with an enrollment of 30 .NCT:NCT07025226OK
